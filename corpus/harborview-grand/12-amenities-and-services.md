# Harborview Grand — Amenities and Guest Services

18 Wharf Street, Portland, ME 04101 · +1-207-555-0100
Last reviewed October 2026.

## Front desk

Staffed **24 hours**. Rooms are ready from 3:00 PM; check-out is by 11:00 AM.

Late check-out until 1:00 PM can usually be arranged on the morning of departure and is subject to
the day's arrivals — ask rather than assume. Luggage storage before check-in and after check-out
is free and does not require a key.

## Breakfast

Served in the harbour-side breakfast room, **6:30 AM to 10:00 AM** on weekdays and **7:00 AM to
11:00 AM** at weekends.

A continental spread is included in every rate: pastries, bread, cereal, yoghurt, fruit, juice,
and coffee. Hot items are available à la carte and charged to the room.

Dogs are not permitted in the breakfast room. A tray can be brought to your room on request at no
charge.

## In-room amenities

Every guest room has Wi-Fi, air conditioning, a television, and a refrigerator. The Accessible
Suite also has a wet bar.

There is no in-room safe at this property. The front desk holds a safe deposit box for valuables
at no charge, available at any hour.

Cooking in guest rooms is not permitted. Refrigerators and the microwave in the breakfast room are
for storage and reheating.

## Wi-Fi

Free throughout the building, including meeting space and the breakfast room. The network is
`Harborview-Guest`; no password is required and no sign-in page appears. Coverage on the balconies
is reliable; coverage in the parking lot is not.

## Laundry

Same-day laundry and dry cleaning Monday to Friday, collected by 9:00 AM and returned by 6:00 PM.
Items collected after 9:00 AM or at weekends are returned the next business day. There is no
self-service laundry on site; the nearest is on Commercial Street, a six-minute walk.

## Business services

Printing, scanning, and copying at the front desk at no charge for reasonable volumes. A desk with
power and wired network is available in the lobby alcove.

Meeting space is bookable by the day — see the Room Type Guide and Meetings and Events.

## Fitness and leisure

There is **no fitness room, pool, or spa** at this property. We have an arrangement with a gym two
blocks away on Fore Street for day passes at a reduced rate; ask the front desk for a voucher.

## Accessibility

Step-free entry from Wharf Street, accessible public toilets on the ground floor, and an
accessible guest room type. The Accessibility Commitment covers this in detail; anything it does
not answer, the front desk will.

## Parking

Limited on-site parking. See Directions and Parking.

## What we do not have

Stated plainly so you can plan: no room service, no bar or restaurant beyond breakfast, no pool,
no fitness room, no airport shuttle, and no in-room safes. Portland's Old Port district is a few
minutes' walk and the front desk will happily book a table.
