# Harborview Grand — Room Type Guide

18 Wharf Street, Portland, ME 04101 · +1-207-555-0100
Rates shown are nightly, before tax and before any special-rate discount.
Last reviewed October 2026.

## Deluxe King, Harbor View

**$249.00 per night · sleeps 2 · one king bed**

Our standard harbour-side room. One king bed, a private balcony overlooking the ferry terminal,
and a seating area by the window.

Amenities: Wi-Fi, air conditioning, television, refrigerator.

Best for couples and single business travellers. This is the room type most often available at
short notice.

## Double Queen, Harbor View

**$279.00 per night · sleeps 4 · two queen beds**

Two queen beds in a larger harbour-side room, with the same balcony and outlook as the Deluxe
King.

Amenities: Wi-Fi, air conditioning, television, refrigerator.

Best for families and for two colleagues travelling together. The stated maximum of four is a
firm limit.

## Accessible Suite, Harbor View

**$329.00 per night · sleeps 4 · one king bed and one sofa bed**

Our largest guest room and the accessible room type at this property. A separate sitting area with
a sofa bed, a king bed, and a wet bar.

Amenities: Wi-Fi, air conditioning, television, refrigerator, wet bar.

Accessibility features include a step-free route from the Wharf Street entrance, a widened
doorway, a roll-in shower with grab bars, lever handles throughout, visual fire-alarm
notification, and controls at reachable heights.

**This room is bookable directly.** It is not held back for telephone booking and is not
re-assigned to guests who did not request it.

## Meeting Room, Harbor View

**$349.00 per day · capacity 20**

A meeting and events space, **booked by the day rather than overnight**. It has no bed and is not
a guest room — the figure above is a day rate and the figure of 20 is a seated capacity, not a
sleeping occupancy.

Amenities: Wi-Fi, television.

The room is accessible, with step-free entry and circulation space sized for a wheelchair. See
Meetings and Events for layouts, catering, and how to book.

## Choosing between them

| If you want | Book |
|-------------|------|
| The lowest harbour-side rate | Deluxe King |
| Two separate beds | Double Queen |
| The most space, or accessibility features | Accessible Suite |
| A room for a meeting rather than a stay | Meeting Room |

All four room types have a harbour outlook. There are no interior-facing rooms at this property.

## What every room includes

Wi-Fi throughout at no charge, air conditioning with in-room controls, a television, and a
refrigerator — except the Meeting Room, which has Wi-Fi and a television only.

Hairdryer, iron, and ironing board are in every guest room. Cots and high chairs are available at
no charge from the front desk, subject to availability; a cot does not increase a room's maximum
occupancy.
