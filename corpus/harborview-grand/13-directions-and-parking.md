# Harborview Grand — Directions and Parking

18 Wharf Street, Portland, ME 04101 · +1-207-555-0100
Last reviewed October 2026.

## Finding us

Wharf Street runs between Commercial Street and Fore Street in Portland's Old Port. We are on the
harbour side, a two-minute walk from the Casco Bay Lines ferry terminal.

![Schematic locator: Harborview Grand on Wharf Street, between Commercial Street and the harbour, with the ferry terminal to the west and the Fore Street Garage to the east.](figures/locator-harborview.png)

Wharf Street is **cobbled and partly pedestrian**. Satellite navigation will sometimes route you
onto the pedestrian section; approach from Commercial Street rather than Fore Street and the
vehicle entrance is on your right.

## From Portland International Jetport (PWM)

Roughly 4 miles, 15 minutes by car outside of peak times.

Taxis and rideshare wait at the terminal and cost $20 to $30. The METRO Route 5 bus runs to the
Portland Transportation Center, where Route 8 continues to Commercial Street; the walk from the
stop is five minutes.

**There is no hotel shuttle.**

## By car

From I-295, take Exit 7 for Franklin Street, continue to Commercial Street, and turn onto Wharf
Street. Allow extra time between June and September — Commercial Street backs up badly on summer
weekends and around ferry departures.

## By train and bus

The Amtrak Downeaster and Concord Coach services both use the Portland Transportation Center, about
2 miles away. Taxi or rideshare takes ten minutes; METRO Route 8 runs to Commercial Street.

## Parking

**On-site parking is limited to 14 spaces and is not guaranteed**, including for guests holding a
reservation. Spaces are released first-come on arrival.

- **Rate:** $28 per night, charged to your folio.
- **Height limit:** 6 feet 6 inches. The lot cannot take a van with a roof rack or a box.
- **Access:** the lot is gated overnight; the front desk issues a card at check-in.

Two accessible spaces are reserved beside the step-free entrance and are held for guests who need
them rather than released first-come. Tell us when you book, or on arrival, and we will hold one.

### When the lot is full

The **Fore Street Garage**, a four-minute walk, is open 24 hours at $22 per night with no height
restriction, and is the alternative we recommend. The Custom House Wharf lot is closer but fills
early and closes at 10:00 PM.

On-street parking on Commercial Street is metered 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM and free overnight and on
Sundays, with a two-hour limit during enforcement.

## Electric vehicles

There are **no charging points on site**. The nearest public chargers are at the Fore Street
Garage, level 2, and at the Ocean Gateway terminal.

## Deliveries and loading

Wharf Street has a loading bay beside the main entrance with a 20-minute limit. Larger deliveries
for meeting space should be arranged with the front desk in advance; we cannot accept freight
without notice.
