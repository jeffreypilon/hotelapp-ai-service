# Harborview Grand — House Rules

18 Wharf Street, Portland, ME 04101 · +1-207-555-0100
Last reviewed October 2026.

## Quiet hours

**10:00 PM to 7:00 AM.** Harborview Grand sits on the working waterfront, a short walk from the
ferry terminal, and sound carries across the harbour side of the building. Televisions, music, and
conversation on balconies should be kept to a level not audible from the corridor or the next room.

Repeated disturbance after a single warning may end a stay without refund.

## Smoking

**The entire property is non-smoking**, including all rooms, balconies, corridors, and meeting
space. This covers cigarettes, cigars, pipes, and vaping devices.

A $250 deep-cleaning charge applies to any room where smoking has occurred. Smoking is permitted
in the designated area at the Wharf Street side of the building.

## Pets

Harborview Grand is **pet-friendly for dogs**, with a limit of two dogs per room and a $35 per-night
fee added to your folio.

Dogs must be leashed in all public areas and may not be left unattended in a room. Dogs are not
permitted in the breakfast room or in meeting space. Guests are responsible for any damage.

Service animals are **not pets**, are welcome everywhere, and are never charged. See the
Accessibility Commitment.

## Guests and visitors

Registered guests may receive visitors in public areas at any time. Visitors to guest rooms must
be signed in at the front desk and must leave by 10:00 PM unless added to the reservation.

Occupancy limits are firm. A room's stated maximum is set by bedding and fire-safety capacity, and
the front desk cannot waive it on arrival.

## Parking

On-site parking is **limited and not guaranteed**. See Directions and Parking for rates,
alternatives, and what to do when the lot is full.

## Rooms and housekeeping

Daily housekeeping is provided between 9:00 AM and 3:00 PM. Hang the privacy card to decline
service for the day.

Please do not move furniture between rooms or remove it from meeting space. Cooking in guest rooms
is not permitted; rooms with a refrigerator and microwave are intended for reheating and storage,
not for preparation.

## Balconies

Harbour-side rooms have balconies. Nothing may be hung from, stored on, or thrown from a balcony,
and balcony doors should be closed when a room is unoccupied — gulls are persistent and will enter
an open room.

## Damage and lost property

Damage beyond normal wear is charged to the folio at cost. Items left behind are held for 30 days
and can be returned at the guest's expense; perishables are disposed of immediately.

## Fire safety

Corridors and stairwells must stay clear. Tampering with a smoke detector, propping a fire door, or
discharging an extinguisher without cause ends a stay immediately and is reported to the Portland
Fire Department.
