# Accessibility Commitment

Applies to all properties. Last reviewed October 2026.

## Booking an accessible room

Accessible rooms are **bookable directly**, like any other room type. You do not need to call
ahead, explain your requirements, or request one as a special favour.

When searching, filter for accessible rooms and only room types with accessibility features will
be shown. Booking one reserves it for you in the same way any other reservation holds a room —
an accessible room booked online is not re-assignable to a guest who did not request one.

This matters, and it is why we built it this way: the common failure in our industry is that
accessible rooms are held back for telephone booking and then given away. Ours are not.

## What "accessible" means in our listings

A room type marked accessible has, at minimum:

- a step-free route from the building entrance to the room;
- a doorway and internal circulation space sized for a wheelchair;
- a bathroom with grab bars and a roll-in or transfer-accessible shower;
- lever-style door handles and tap fittings;
- visual as well as audible fire alarm notification;
- controls, switches, and the in-room safe at reachable heights.

Specific features vary between our properties and between room types. Each property's own
Accessibility and Room Type documents list exactly what that property provides, and the front desk
will answer anything those documents do not.

## Meeting and event spaces

Accessible meeting space is treated the same way: where a meeting room is listed as accessible,
the route, entry, and circulation space meet the standards above.

## Service animals

Service animals are welcome throughout both properties, including in rooms, dining areas, and
meeting space, and at no charge. This is separate from our pet policy, which does not apply to
service animals.

We do not ask for documentation or require advance notice. Telling us when you book simply lets
us put you in a room near an exit if that would be easier, which some guests prefer and others
would rather we did not assume.

## Hearing and vision

Visual fire-alarm notification is fitted in accessible rooms at both properties. Portable
notification kits — door-knock and telephone alerters — are available on request at either
property, subject to availability; ask at least 24 hours ahead where you can.

Large-print copies of our guest directory and menus are available at the front desk.

## Mobility equipment

Guests are welcome to bring and store mobility equipment. Charging a powered wheelchair or scooter
in your room is fine. Equipment may not be left in corridors or stairwells, which is a fire-exit
requirement rather than a preference; the front desk will find a secure place to store anything
that will not fit in the room.

## Telling us something is wrong

If something described here is not true of your stay, tell the front desk during the stay rather
than after it. We would far rather fix it while you are still with us.
