# Booking Terms and Conditions

Applies to all properties. Last reviewed October 2026.

## Making a reservation

Reservations are made by **date**, not by time of day. You select a check-in date and a
check-out date, and the number of nights is the difference between them. A stay from 14 November
to 17 November is three nights; you are not charged for the check-out date.

The check-out date must be after the check-in date. Same-day reservations — where check-in and
check-out fall on the same date — are not accepted, because they describe a zero-night stay.

Reservations cannot be made for dates in the past.

## Confirmation numbers

Every confirmed reservation receives a confirmation number beginning with `HA`, followed by eight
characters — for example, `HA7K2M9QX4`. Quote it in any correspondence. It is the fastest way for
staff to find your booking, and the only identifier you need to keep.

## Occupancy limits

Each room type has a stated maximum occupancy, and it is a limit rather than a guideline. It
reflects bedding, floor area, and fire-safety capacity for that room.

Children count toward occupancy regardless of age. If your party exceeds the maximum for a room
type, book an additional room or a larger type — the front desk cannot waive the limit on arrival,
and a party that exceeds it may be asked to take a second room at the rate available that night.

Meeting and conference spaces state a **capacity** rather than an occupancy, because they are
booked by the day rather than for overnight stay.

## Arrival and departure

Rooms are available from **3:00 PM** on the check-in date. Check-out is by **11:00 AM** on the
departure date.

These are property operating times, not part of the reservation itself — your booking covers
dates, and the hours above describe when a room is ready and when it must be vacated. Early
arrival and late departure can often be accommodated and are never guaranteed; ask the front desk
on the day.

## Payment

Payment is taken at the time of booking. See the Payment and Billing Policy for accepted methods
and what happens when a payment is declined.

## Identification

The guest named on the reservation must present government-issued photo identification at
check-in, matching the reservation name. We cannot release a room to a third party without prior
written authorisation from the named guest.

## Changes to these terms

These terms are the ones in force at the time your reservation is made. A later revision does not
alter a booking already confirmed.

## Right to refuse

We may decline or terminate a stay where a guest's conduct endangers staff or other guests,
where the property is misused, or where a reservation is made fraudulently. The property's House
Rules set out expected conduct in detail.
