# Cancellation and Rate-Type Policy

Applies to all properties. Last reviewed October 2026.

## The standard cancellation window

Reservations on our Flexible rate may be cancelled **up to 48 hours before check-in** with a
full refund.

The deadline is a fixed 48-hour duration measured back from the start of your check-in date **in
the property's own local time**, not in your time zone and not in the time zone you booked from.
Both Harborview Grand and Lakeside Inn operate on Eastern Time.

A worked example. For a check-in date of 14 November at Harborview Grand, the local check-in
instant is 14 November at 00:00 Eastern. Forty-eight hours earlier is **12 November at 00:00
Eastern**. A cancellation submitted at any point before that instant is refundable; one submitted
after it is not.

Because the window is a fixed 48-hour duration rather than "midnight two days before," it moves
with daylight saving transitions. A stay that begins just after the spring-forward change has a
deadline exactly 48 hours earlier by the clock, which may read as a different wall-clock hour than
you expect. The confirmation email always shows the exact deadline for your reservation.

## Cancelling after the deadline

**You may still cancel.** We do not lock a reservation once the window has closed.

A cancellation made inside the 48-hour window is simply **non-refundable** — the reservation moves
to cancelled status and the room is released, but no refund is issued. We would rather you tell us
you are not coming than have the room sit empty, so the option stays open until the stay begins.

Cancelling a reservation that has already been cancelled, or one you have already checked into, is
not possible. If you believe a reservation's status is wrong, contact the property directly.

## Changing dates instead of cancelling

Date changes follow the **same 48-hour rule**. You may modify your dates freely up to 48 hours
before check-in, subject to availability for the new dates. Inside the window, dates are fixed.

A date change re-prices the stay at the rates in effect for the new dates. If the new dates are
more expensive, the difference is due; if they are cheaper, the difference is refunded. The
cancellation deadline is recalculated from the **new** check-in date, which can move it either
earlier or later.

## Rate types and what they mean

Cancellation terms attach to the **rate you booked**, not to the property. Two guests in identical
rooms on the same night may hold different cancellation terms.

| Rate type | Cancellation |
|-----------|--------------|
| Flexible | Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in |
| Advance Purchase | **Non-refundable from the moment of booking.** Offered at a reduced nightly rate in exchange for that certainty |
| Group and event blocks | Governed by the signed group agreement, which supersedes this policy |

If you are unsure which rate you hold, your confirmation shows it. When in doubt, assume the
stricter terms and call the property.

## Length of stay

A single reservation may cover a maximum of **30 nights**. Longer stays are arranged as
consecutive reservations or through a group agreement; the front desk can set either up.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Our side of the process completes immediately
on cancellation; your bank or card issuer typically takes a further 5 to 10 business days to post
the credit, which is outside our control.

## No-shows

A reservation where the guest does not arrive and has not cancelled is treated as a cancellation
inside the window: the room is released and the stay is non-refundable.
