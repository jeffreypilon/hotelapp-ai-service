# Special Rates and Discounts

Applies to all properties. Last reviewed October 2026.

## How special rates work

A special rate is a **percentage discount off the nightly rate**, set by each property for each
category it chooses to honour. It is applied automatically when you select the category during
your search — there is no code to type and no coupon to redeem.

Two things follow from this that guests sometimes find surprising:

1. **A category is not honoured everywhere.** Each property decides which categories it offers and
   at what percentage. A rate available at one of our properties may not be available at the other.
2. **Where a category is not offered, your search still works.** You will simply see the standard
   nightly rate rather than a discounted one. Nothing is hidden from you and nothing fails.

## Categories

| Category | Who it is for |
|----------|---------------|
| AAA / CAA | Members of AAA or CAA, on presentation of a valid membership card at check-in |
| AARP | AARP members |
| Senior | Guests aged 60 and over |
| Military / Veteran | Active-duty service members and veterans |
| Government per diem | Government travellers on official business, at the published per-diem rate |
| Corporate | Guests travelling under a negotiated corporate agreement |
| Group | Guests booking within an arranged group block |

Selecting no category gives the standard rate, which is always available to everyone.

## Current availability

**Harborview Grand** offers a **10% AAA / CAA discount**.

**Lakeside Inn** does not currently offer a special-rate discount. Standard rates apply to all
bookings.

Offered categories and percentages change from time to time. The rate shown in your search results
at the moment of booking is the rate you pay.

## Proof of eligibility

Eligibility is verified at **check-in**, not at booking. Bring the relevant membership card,
identification, or orders.

A guest who cannot produce proof of eligibility for a discounted rate will be asked to pay the
difference between the discounted rate and the standard rate for the stay. We would rather tell
you this plainly here than surprise you at the desk.

## How the discount is calculated

The discount applies to the **nightly** rate, which is then rounded to the cent and multiplied by
the number of nights.

For example: a $249.00 nightly rate with a 10% AAA discount gives $224.10 per night. Over three
nights that is $672.30.

The per-night figure is rounded before multiplication, not after. On most stays the two approaches
agree; on some they differ by a cent, and the per-night figure shown on your confirmation is
authoritative.

## Combining discounts

Special rates **cannot be combined**. One category applies to a reservation, and selecting a
category replaces rather than stacks with any other.

If you qualify for more than one, choose the larger. Where two categories are offered at the same
percentage, it makes no difference which you select.

## Group and corporate agreements

Group blocks and negotiated corporate rates are arranged directly with the property and are
governed by the signed agreement, which may set its own cancellation terms that supersede our
standard policy. Contact the property to set one up.
