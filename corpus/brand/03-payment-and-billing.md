# Payment and Billing Policy

Applies to all properties. Last reviewed October 2026.

## When payment is taken

Payment is collected **at the time of booking**, not on arrival. A reservation is not confirmed
until payment succeeds; if it does not, no room is held and no confirmation number is issued.

This is deliberate. Holding inventory against an unpaid booking takes rooms out of availability
for guests who are ready to book, so we confirm and charge together.

## Accepted payment methods

Major credit and debit cards. We require:

- the full card number;
- the cardholder's name as printed on the card;
- the expiry month and year;
- the card security code (CVV).

We do not accept cash, cheque, or bank transfer for online reservations. Cash is accepted at the
property for incidental charges during a stay.

## Declined payments

If your card is declined, the reservation is **not** created and the room is not held. Nothing is
charged. The usual causes are an expired card, an incorrect security code, insufficient funds, or
a block placed by your issuer on an unfamiliar merchant.

Correct the details and try again, or use a different card. If a decline repeats with a card you
believe is good, your issuer is the right first call — we receive only the decline, not the
reason for it.

## Currency

All rates are quoted and charged in **US dollars**. If your card is denominated in another
currency, your issuer performs the conversion at its own rate and may add a foreign-transaction
fee. The amount we charge is the dollar amount shown at booking.

## Taxes and fees

Rates shown are the nightly room rate. Applicable state and local lodging taxes are calculated on
the total and shown before you confirm. We do not charge resort fees, destination fees, or
booking fees — the total you see before confirming is the total you pay.

## What the total covers

The reservation total is the nightly rate multiplied by the number of nights, with any special-rate
discount applied to the nightly rate first.

The order matters and is worth stating: the discount is applied to the nightly rate and rounded to
the cent, **then** multiplied by the number of nights. Discounting the full stay total instead
would produce a different figure by a cent or two on some stays, and the per-night figure shown on
your confirmation is the authoritative one.

## Incidental charges

Charges incurred during a stay — parking, damage, items from the room — are settled at the front
desk on departure and are separate from the reservation total.

## Refunds

Refunds arising from a cancellation follow the Cancellation and Rate-Type Policy. They are issued
to the original payment method; we cannot refund to a different card.

Our processing completes at the moment of cancellation. The credit typically posts to your account
within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your issuer.

## Receipts

An itemised receipt is available from your reservation details at any time after booking, and is
emailed on check-out. Folios for business travel can be reissued by the front desk on request.
