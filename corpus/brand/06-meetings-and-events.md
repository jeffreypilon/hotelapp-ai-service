# Meetings and Events

Last reviewed October 2026.

## Where we have meeting space

**Harborview Grand, Portland** has one meeting room: the **Meeting Room, Harbor View**, capacity
20, at **$349.00 per day**.

**Lakeside Inn has no meeting space.** Enquiries for Burlington are referred to Harborview Grand
or to a local venue; the desk will help either way.

## How meeting space is booked

The Meeting Room is booked **by the day**, not overnight.

This distinction matters more than it sounds. Every other space in our properties is a guest room
sold by the night and described by how many people can sleep in it. The Meeting Room is sold by
the day and described by how many people can **sit** in it — the figure of 20 is a seated
capacity, there is no bed, and nobody stays the night in it.

A booking runs 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM on the day. Evening use to 10:00 PM can be arranged in advance
for an additional half-day charge.

## Capacity by layout

Capacity 20 assumes a boardroom layout. Other arrangements hold fewer:

| Layout | Capacity |
|--------|----------|
| Boardroom | 20 |
| Theatre | 20 |
| U-shape | 14 |
| Classroom | 12 |
| Cabaret rounds | 12 |

![The four layouts, left to right: boardroom seats 20, theatre seats 20, U-shape seats 14, classroom seats 12.](figures/meeting-layouts.png)

Tell us the layout when you book and the room will be set before you arrive. Rearranging on the
day is possible but takes about 30 minutes.

## What is included

Wi-Fi, a wall-mounted television with HDMI, a whiteboard with pens, and still water.

Available at no charge on request: flip chart, extension leads, and a conference speakerphone.
Printing and copying at the front desk for reasonable volumes.

**We do not provide** laptops, adapters for non-HDMI outputs, video-conference cameras, or
technical support during a session. Bring your own adapter — this is the single most common thing
guests are caught out by.

## Catering

Continental breakfast, sandwich lunch, and a tea and coffee service can be arranged with at least
**72 hours' notice**. Dietary requirements are straightforward with that much warning and
difficult without it.

Outside catering is permitted by prior arrangement. Alcohol may not be brought onto the property
for an event.

## Accessibility

The Meeting Room is accessible: step-free entry from the Wharf Street entrance, a widened doorway,
and circulation space sized for a wheelchair. Accessible toilets are on the same floor.

Tell us about hearing, vision, or mobility requirements when you book and we will set the room to
suit — a U-shape with wider gaps, a seat nearest the door, a position with a clear sightline for
lipreading.

## Cancellation

Meeting-space bookings follow the standard **48-hour** rule in the Cancellation and Rate-Type
Policy: free cancellation up to 48 hours before the booked day, non-refundable inside it.

Catering ordered is charged in full if cancelled inside 48 hours, because it has already been
bought.

Group blocks that combine meeting space with guest rooms are governed by the signed group
agreement, which sets its own terms and supersedes this policy.

## Booking

Contact Harborview Grand directly on +1-207-555-0100. Meeting space is not bookable through the
online guest search, which sells overnight accommodation.
