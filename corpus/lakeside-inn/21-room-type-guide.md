# Lakeside Inn — Room Type Guide

7 North Shore Road, Burlington, VT 05401 · +1-802-555-0177
Rates shown are nightly, before tax.
Last reviewed October 2026.

Lakeside Inn has **twenty-four rooms across two room types**. We are a small property and we do
not have suites, meeting space, or interconnecting rooms.

## Classic King, Lakeside Inn

**$229.00 per night · sleeps 2 · one king bed**

One king bed in a room facing either the lake or the gardens. Lake-facing rooms are assigned on
request where available and carry no surcharge — tell the desk when you book and we will do our
best.

Amenities: Wi-Fi, air conditioning, television, refrigerator.

Best for couples. This is our most common room and the one most likely to be free at short notice.

## Double Queen, Lakeside Inn

**$259.00 per night · sleeps 4 · two queen beds**

Two queen beds in a larger room. Most Double Queens are in the newer wing, which has the better
sound insulation.

Amenities: Wi-Fi, air conditioning, television, refrigerator.

Best for families and for friends travelling together. The maximum of four is firm.

## Accessibility — what we can and cannot offer

**Lakeside Inn does not currently have an accessible room type.** We would rather say so plainly
here than have you discover it on arrival.

The main building dates from 1912. Guest rooms are reached by the original staircase, there is no
lift, and the bathrooms cannot take a roll-in shower without structural work we have not yet done.

What we do have: a step-free entrance from the car park into the lobby and breakfast room, an
accessible ground-floor public toilet, step-free access to the porch and lawn, and a firm,
compacted path to the dock.

Guests who need an accessible guest room should book our **Portland property, Harborview Grand**,
whose Accessible Suite has a roll-in shower, grab bars, a widened doorway, and visual fire-alarm
notification. The desk at either property can move a booking across.

Service animals are welcome here without restriction.

## What every room includes

Wi-Fi, air conditioning with in-room controls, a television, and a refrigerator.

Hairdryer and iron in every room. Cots are available at no charge from the desk, subject to
availability; a cot does not raise a room's maximum occupancy.

There are no in-room safes. The desk will hold valuables.

Lake towels are issued at the desk and are different from room towels — please use those for the
dock.
