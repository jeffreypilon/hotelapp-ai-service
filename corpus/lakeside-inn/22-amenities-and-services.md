# Lakeside Inn — Amenities and Guest Services

7 North Shore Road, Burlington, VT 05401 · +1-802-555-0177
Last reviewed October 2026.

## Front desk

Staffed **7:00 AM to 10:00 PM**. We are not a 24-hour property.

Arriving after 10:00 PM is fine, and needs five minutes of planning: tell us your approximate
arrival time and we will leave a key in the lockbox by the main door with the code sent to you on
the day. The night line reaches the duty manager for anything urgent.

Rooms are ready from 3:00 PM; check-out is by 11:00 AM. Late check-out to 1:00 PM is usually
possible except at weekends in July and August. Luggage storage before and after is free.

## Breakfast

Served in the breakfast room overlooking the lawn, **7:30 AM to 9:30 AM daily** — a narrower
window than our Portland property, so set an alarm.

Included in every rate: eggs cooked to order, local bread and pastries, oats, yoghurt, fruit, and
Vermont maple syrup that is genuinely from down the road. Coffee is out from 6:30 AM for early
risers.

Dietary requirements are easy for us at this size — tell us the night before and we will have
something right for you.

## The lake

A private dock, a small shingle beach, and lawn running down to the water.

**Swimming is unsupervised at all times.** The dock is closed dusk to 8:00 AM. Lake towels are
issued at the desk; please use those rather than room towels.

Two kayaks and a paddleboard are available free to guests on a first-come basis, with buoyancy
aids in the dock box. Sign out at the desk. Lake Champlain is cold until late June and the north
shore gets a stiff afternoon wind — check with us before taking a kayak out.

## In-room amenities

Wi-Fi, air conditioning, television, and a refrigerator in every room. No in-room safes; the desk
holds valuables at no charge.

## Wi-Fi

Free throughout the main building and on the porches. The network is `LakesideInn-Guest` and the
password is on your key card.

Coverage reaches the near lawn but **not the dock or the beach**, which is partly geography and
partly deliberate.

## Laundry

No laundry or dry-cleaning service, and no self-service machines on site. A laundromat on North
Avenue is a seven-minute drive. We will lend an iron and a drying rack for small items.

## Business services

Printing and copying at the desk for small volumes. **There is no meeting space at this property**
— Harborview Grand in Portland has a 20-capacity meeting room bookable by the day.

## Parking

**Free, on site, and unreserved** — ample for all twenty-four rooms. No permit or card needed.
This is a real difference from our Portland property, where parking is limited and charged.

## Accessibility

Step-free entry from the car park to the lobby, breakfast room, porch, and an accessible
ground-floor toilet. **There is no accessible guest room and no lift** — see the Room Type Guide
for what that means and what we suggest instead.

## What we do not have

Said plainly: no lift, no accessible guest room, no restaurant beyond breakfast, no bar, no pool,
no fitness room, no meeting space, no laundry service, no 24-hour desk, and no pets. Burlington's
Church Street Marketplace is ten minutes away and has all of the eating and drinking.
