# Lakeside Inn — House Rules

7 North Shore Road, Burlington, VT 05401 · +1-802-555-0177
Last reviewed October 2026.

## Quiet hours

**9:00 PM to 8:00 AM**, an hour earlier and an hour later than our Portland property. Lakeside Inn
is twenty-four rooms in a residential stretch of the north shore, with neighbours either side and
single-glazed windows in the original building. Sound carries.

Please keep conversation on the porches and the lawn low after 9:00 PM. This is the rule guests
most often forget on a warm evening and the one our neighbours notice most.

## Smoking

**The entire property is non-smoking**, indoors and out, including the porches, the lawn, and the
dock. This covers cigarettes, cigars, pipes, and vaping devices.

The buildings are timber-framed and over a century old; this is a fire rule, not a preference. A
$250 deep-cleaning charge applies to any room where smoking has occurred, and a stay may be ended
without refund.

There is no designated smoking area on the property. The nearest permitted spot is the public
path at the North Shore Road end of the drive.

## Pets

**Lakeside Inn does not accept pets.** We are a small property with shared porches and a breakfast
room that opens onto the lawn, and we cannot separate guests who would rather not be near animals.

Guests travelling with a dog are welcome at our Portland property, which is pet-friendly.

**Service animals are not pets.** They are welcome throughout Lakeside Inn, at no charge, with no
documentation required and no advance notice needed. See the Accessibility Commitment.

## The lake, the dock, and the lawn

Guests swim and use the dock **at their own risk**. There is no lifeguard at any time.

The dock is closed from dusk until 8:00 AM. Children under 14 must be accompanied by an adult on
the dock and in the water at all times.

Lawn furniture stays on the lawn. Please do not take towels, chairs, or blankets from the rooms
down to the water — ask at the desk and we will give you lake towels, which is what they are for.

No open fires, charcoal grills, or fireworks anywhere on the property, including the shore.

## Guests and visitors

Visitors are welcome in the public areas until 9:00 PM. Visitors to guest rooms must be signed in
at the desk.

Occupancy limits are firm, set by bedding and fire-safety capacity, and cannot be waived on
arrival.

## Rooms and housekeeping

Housekeeping runs 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Hang the privacy card to skip a day.

Cooking in rooms is not permitted. The refrigerator in each room is for storage.

Please remove wet swimwear and lake shoes before going up the main staircase — the treads are
original and slippery when wet.

## Damage and lost property

Damage beyond normal wear is charged at cost. Items left behind are held for 30 days; we will post
them at your expense.

## Fire safety

Corridors and the main staircase must stay clear. Candles, incense, and any open flame are
prohibited in guest rooms. Tampering with a detector or propping a fire door ends a stay
immediately.
