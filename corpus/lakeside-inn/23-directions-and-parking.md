# Lakeside Inn — Directions and Parking

7 North Shore Road, Burlington, VT 05401 · +1-802-555-0177
Last reviewed October 2026.

## Finding us

North Shore Road runs along the lake north of Burlington's centre. We are the white 1912 building
set back behind a gravel drive, with the lawn running down to the water.

![Schematic locator: Lakeside Inn set back from North Shore Road on the lake side, with North Avenue inland, the Island Line Trail to the north and North Beach nearby.](figures/locator-lakeside.png)

The drive entrance is **easy to miss**. Watch for the low stone wall and the sign on the lake side
of the road; if you reach the junction with Institute Road you have gone about 200 yards too far.
There is room to turn at the junction.

## From Burlington International Airport (BTV)

Roughly 6 miles, 15 to 20 minutes by car.

Taxis and rideshare are at the terminal and cost $25 to $35. Green Mountain Transit Route 1 runs
to downtown Burlington, where Route 20 continues north; the walk from the nearest stop is about
ten minutes along North Shore Road, which has a footpath for part of the way but not all of it.

**There is no hotel shuttle.** If you are arriving after dark without a car, take a taxi.

## By car

From I-89, take Exit 14W onto US-2 toward Burlington, continue to North Avenue, and follow it
north to North Shore Road.

In winter, North Shore Road is ploughed but is not a priority route and can be slippery after an
overnight fall. The drive is gravel and is ploughed by us, usually by 7:00 AM.

## By train and bus

Amtrak's Ethan Allen Express serves Burlington Union Station downtown, about 10 minutes away by
taxi. Intercity buses use the downtown transit centre.

## Parking

**Free, on site, unreserved, and ample** — there is room for every room to have a car, and we have
never run out. No permit, card, or sign-in is needed; park anywhere on the gravel.

This differs from our Portland property, where parking is limited, charged, and not guaranteed. If
you are visiting both, do not assume Portland works the same way.

Two spaces nearest the step-free lobby entrance are marked and are kept for guests who need them.

The gravel surface is firm and compacted but is not paved, which is worth knowing if you use a
wheelchair or a walking frame — the path from those two spaces to the lobby door is the smoothest
route and is the one we maintain in winter.

There is no height restriction. Vans, roof boxes, and small trailers are all fine; tell us if you
are bringing a boat trailer so we can point you somewhere sensible.

## Electric vehicles

**No charging points on site.** The nearest public chargers are at the North Avenue shopping area,
about four minutes by car, and there are several downtown.

## Deliveries

Deliveries come to the main door. We are a small property and can usually take a parcel for an
arriving guest — tell us to expect it.
