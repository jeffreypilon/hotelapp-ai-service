# Lakeside Inn — Burlington Area Guide

7 North Shore Road, Burlington, VT 05401 · +1-802-555-0177
Last reviewed October 2026.

Distances are from our drive. Most things here need a car or a bus; unlike our Portland property,
we are not in the middle of town.

## Lake Champlain

The lake is at the bottom of our lawn, and it is the reason to stay here.

It is 120 miles long, deep, and cold — swimmable from late June to early September, bracing either
side of that. The Adirondacks are the mountains across the water; the sunsets behind them are the
thing guests photograph most.

**Our dock and beach** are private to guests. Kayaks and a paddleboard are free to borrow — see
Amenities and Guest Services.

**North Beach**, a public beach with a snack bar and showers, is five minutes by car.

## Downtown Burlington

Ten minutes by car, or Route 20 then Route 1 by bus.

**Church Street Marketplace** is four pedestrianised blocks of restaurants, bars, and shops, and
is where to go for dinner. It is busy and good year-round, and especially in summer evenings.

**The Burlington waterfront** has a boathouse, boat tours, and the ECHO science centre, which is
the right call with children on a wet afternoon.

## Cycling and walking

The **Island Line Trail** is the headline. A flat, former-railbed path running north along the
lake, including a three-mile causeway built straight out across the water with lake on both
sides. The bike ferry across the Cut operates summer into early autumn.

The trail is a five-minute ride from our drive. Bike hire is available downtown and at several
points along the trail; we do not hire bikes ourselves.

The **Burlington Bike Path** runs south from downtown along the waterfront for eight miles.

## Food and drink beyond ours

Vermont does this well, and most of it needs a short drive.

- **Breweries** — several within twenty minutes, including some with national reputations. Book
  tours ahead in summer.
- **Farm stands and cheesemakers** — plentiful along Route 7 south and in the Champlain Valley.
- **Maple** — sugarhouses within half an hour; sugaring season is late February to early April and
  is worth planning a visit around.

## Seasons

**Summer** is the reason most guests come: warm, long evenings, the lake swimmable, the trail
busy. July and August book out well ahead.

**Autumn** peaks late September into mid-October and is spectacular here. Book early; this is our
busiest fortnight.

**Winter** is cold and quiet, with skiing at Stowe and Smugglers' Notch about 45 minutes away. The
lake sometimes freezes near shore; **do not walk on it** — ice on Champlain is unreliable and
every local will tell you the same.

**Spring** is mud season: quiet, cheap, and the trails are poor until late May.

## Day trips

**Stowe** — 45 minutes. Skiing in winter, hiking and the gondola in summer.
**Shelburne Museum** — 20 minutes. 39 buildings of Americana, open seasonally.
**Montreal** — about 2 hours, international border. Bring your passport.

## Practical

**Grocery and pharmacy** — North Avenue, 4 minutes by car; the pharmacy closes at 8:00 PM.
**Urgent care** — UVM Medical Center, 12 minutes. Emergencies, dial 911.
**Mobile coverage** — good at the inn, patchy along parts of the Island Line causeway.
